FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Accuracy How accurate is Zaraa's AI diagnosis?

Zaraa leverages a dual-stage AI architecture that first identifies the plant species and then analyzes the leaf image to detect diseases, delivering reliable diagnosis results and personalized treatment recommendations.

Setup Do I need the robot to use Zaraa?

No. You can start by uploading photos from your phone. The robot is an optional add-on for automated field capture.

Crops Which crops are supported?

Zaraa currently supports plant species such as Apple, Cherry, Corn, Tomato, Grape, Peach, Peper, Potato, and Strawberry. After identifying the plant, the AI analyzes the leaf image for diseases and provides personalized treatment recommendations.

Privacy Is my data private?

Yes. Images and diagnoses are encrypted in transit and at rest, and never shared with third parties.

Marketplace Can I sell products in the marketplace?

Verified sellers can list seeds, fertilizers, organic products and equipment.
